About Medical S.C.R system

This is a Medical Specialist Consultation and Referral System developed to bridge the gap between patients and healthcare specialists through a secure online platform.

The system enables patients to search for medical specialists, view their professional profiles, book consultations, and receive expert healthcare services remotely.

Specialists can create and manage their profiles by providing information about their qualifications, specialization, experience, and contact details. This helps patients identify the most suitable healthcare professional for their medical needs.

Our goal is to improve healthcare accessibility, enhance collaboration among healthcare professionals, and ensure that patients receive quality medical care regardless of their location.
